Who We Are

Nivaran Dx is an infectious disease diagnostics group dedicated to engineering accessible, affordable, and field-deployable point-of-care (PoC) testing technologies. Operating at the intersection of translational nanobiotechnology, analytical chemistry, and biomedical instrumentation, our group focuses on solving critical bottlenecks in infectious disease detection—moving complex molecular and biomarker assays out of centralized reference laboratories and directly to the frontlines of patient care.

By integrating simplified sample preparation, robust nanomaterial and molecular chemistry, and compact semi-automated hardware, we aim to empower frontline clinicians and community health workers with rapid, reliable diagnostic answers.

B Why We Started

Our Inspiration

Closing the diagnostic infrastructure gap between India’s metros and its rural heartland.

“A patient’s chance at early diagnosis should never depend on their pin code”

Across India, a stark tale of two healthcare worlds persists. In major metropolitan cities, tertiary hospitals are equipped with automated molecular laboratories capable of detecting viral infections within hours. Yet, just a few hours away in rural districts, small towns, and primary health centres, millions of families face an overwhelming diagnostic void—where basic confirmatory testing requires travelling long distances, losing days of daily wages, and waiting weeks for samples to be transported to distant city labs.

For silent, progressive infections like Hepatitis C and acute febrile illnesses, this delay is devastating. Too often, patients are lost to follow-up before their test results ever return, turning treatable infections into life-threatening complications. Witnessing this human cost firsthand inspired the founding of Nivaran Dx (from the Sanskrit “Nivaran”, meaning resolution and relief)—with a singular commitment to re-engineer high-precision diagnostics so they work reliably at the community clinic bedside, without cold chains, expensive thermal cyclers, or specialized lab infrastructure.

Semi-Automated Point-of-Care (PoC) Platform for Early HCV Detection

Inspired by decentralized point-of-care molecular diagnostics, our flagship platform is a compact, semi-automated device engineered for rapid, early detection of Hepatitis C Virus (HCV). Designed to bridge the gap between rapid antibody screening and centralized RT-PCR confirmation, the system streamlines sample processing and optical readout into an intuitive, low-cost workflow suitable for district hospitals and rural primary health centres.

1 Sample Input

Micro-volume patient sample loaded into disposable reaction cartridge/tube.

2 Semi-Automated Assay

Controlled onboard processing without bulky PCR thermal cyclers.

3 Rapid Readout

Clear, objective result enabling same-visit clinical linkage to care.
